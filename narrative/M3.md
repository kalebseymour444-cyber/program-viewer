## Seam notes

**Overhead space is the contested one.** Cable tray competes with mechanical pipe and electrical
busway for the same volume, and whoever hangs steel last does it twice. Resolve the overhead model on
site with all three trades present, before anyone pulls cable. This is a classic seam where each trade
is executing its own scope correctly and the aggregate still fails.

**Certification is not testing.** Testing tells you the link comes up. Certification measures against
a loss budget and produces evidence. Only the second survives a dispute, which is why the certification
package is a payment milestone rather than a status update.

**Labels are an interface, not decoration.** The label scheme has to reconcile with what monitoring
will report, so that a logical alarm resolves to a physical cable without a human translating between
two naming schemes at two in the morning.
